Release notes

V4.0

18 November 2026

Release on GitHub

New Features

  • 「 Fate 」 (Agency pick, off by default): When you try something risky that could change the scene, hidden dice decide it, even when you write it as a success. Sure things and calm turns get no roll. The model sets your odds, carries the action to the instant before it lands and writes both outcomes. A roll it never sees keeps one, and a small note under it shows the roll, what it needed, and a pass in gold or a fail in red. A failure costs you something, so the story moves on either way. For models that let you win every time. Needs ! Fate Outcomes !, which is on by default, and the Preset setup card warns you when it's off. On Lumiverse, pick it under Agency in Configure Prompt Variables
  • | Diaries | (off by default): The two characters closest to you keep a private diary. After a scene that changed how they see you, they write the moment down in their own voice and recall it later. Entries show their feelings even when thoughts are 「 Hidden 」. The diary gets its own tab in the tray
  • 🔻PICK: (+18) GORE & VIOLENCE🔻 (off by default): A new pick for how violence looks. 「 Realistic Gore 」 writes wounds matched to the weapon and force, and injuries keep limiting what a character can do. 「 Ultraviolence 」 goes over the top with blood and spatter while the wounds stay what the attack made them. Kimi K2.6 writes it in full, and GLM 5.2 stays close to 「 Realistic Gore 」. 「 Nothing Graphic 」 keeps fights, injuries and deaths with real stakes and keeps wounds brief, like a PG-13 film. How often violence happens stays with the story
  • | New Faces | (off by default): The first time a character appears in the story, a card records who they are: name, appearance, background, personality, speech, goal and skills, with secrets marked. Recurring characters get a full card, passing ones a single line. Until you hear or see a name, the card's title, the narration, thought labels and footers call the character by description ("the barkeep"), and a secret name never shows in a title. Characters who already have a sheet get none, and no one is invented to fill the footer
  • ! Pinned Cards !: An empty entry where you paste the | New Faces | cards you want to keep, so those characters survive summaries and long chats. Each card has a copy button. On Lumiverse it's in Configure Prompt Variables → Utilities
  • | Dark Portrayals ☢️ | (off by default): For models that keep every story polite and wholesome. Among the outcomes the story supports, the darker one happens: threats are carried out, injuries, grief and broken trust last, and cruel characters stay cruel. Warm and quiet beats still have room, for contrast
  • Phi's Take: A new ! Scene Plan ! row where Phi says what moves, worries or amuses her in the moment, and what the prose should linger on. Her taste now shows in what the story dwells on, and she's fond of her characters
  • Companions 「 Notes 」: Planned in the ! Scene Plan !, so each Companion reacts to a new detail every turn
  • Self-repairing replies: When the model doubles a block's opening tag or leaves a block unclosed, Phi's notes included, the saved reply is repaired automatically and the model reads the repaired version, so one broken reply no longer becomes a habit. A Tracker or footer left open when a reply is cut off or stopped still shows as its card, and footers now show as cards while they stream. The response order also names each block by its tag and asks for every block to be opened once and closed before the next
  • 「 Gemini Braille 」 (experimental, off by default): For Gemini 4 Argon. The model writes Braille blanks in place of spaces, which may help replies past Google's filter, and DEM turns them back into spaces. Replies and chat history cost about 50% more tokens. 「 Gemini 」 is renamed 「 Gemini Instructions 」

Fixed/Improved

  • Sex scenes: Build slowly over many turns and linger in each act. The scene ends only when your turn ends it. The model never writes your climax, and never assumes you're close to orgasm unless you say so
  • Consent in sex scenes: The ! Scene Plan ! no longer asks about consent every turn (「 Realistic Smut 」 and 「 Max Lewdness 」), so characters stop checking for a yes at each step. It plans what each character wants and how it shows, and consent follows the character and the situation
  • Talk during sex: Characters are less chatty. How much each one talks follows the character, from steady dirty talk to breath and sounds only, and the ! Scene Plan ! decides each turn who speaks and who stays silent (「 Realistic Smut 」 and 「 Max Lewdness 」)
  • 「 Realistic Smut 」: Uses explicit terms freely. Characters may want and start position changes
  • 「 Max Lewdness 」: Characters eagerly push for new positions, angles, locations, roles and commands. Teasing and edging stretch over many turns
  • 「 Cinematic 」 and 「 Dry 」: No longer skip transitions to speed the story along
  • 「 You control {{user}} 」: Strength, skill, resistance and odds decide how your attempts land. A push that fails stays failed until your next turn
  • | Content Policy |: No longer treats every story as dark. When a scene holds violence, cruelty or sex, it's written fully and explicitly, unless you pick 「 Nothing Graphic 」
  • 「 Flexible 」 length: Longer replies: about 250 words for quick exchanges, 300-450 for most scenes, up to 550 for complex ones
  • Prose Length: The ! Scene Plan ! weighs the type of scene before picking a length, instead of copying the option's range every turn
  • | Status |: A clear test for who counts as major: likely to return, or holding a goal, secret or tie to {{user}}. Everyone else is left out, and characters who stop being major are removed
  • 「 Undercurrents 」: Every route grows out of the scene, the character sheets or past events. Characters don't take your silence as an answer to their questions or invitations
  • Option combinations: Pairings whose rules clashed now say which one wins: 「 Friendship 」 + 「 Max Lewdness 」, 「 Visible 」 thoughts + 「 With Asterisks 」 (italics no longer break), 「 Short 」 + 「 Input Improver 」 or 「 Choices List 」, Companions 「 Notes 」 + | Phi's Scratchpad |, | Anti-Positivity Bias | + 「 Suspenseful 」, and Narrative Styles with a Narration option
  • | Banned Terms |: Bans "load-bearing". The semicolon and colon ban leaves markup and clock times like 08:22 alone. Names the card already uses are never treated as banned
  • | Anti-Repetition |: Plain mentions of names, places and times are allowed
  • 「 Hidden 」 thoughts: Stay hidden while the reply streams, and when it's cut off mid-thought
  • OOC: Phi's answer comes first, before the Scene Plan
  • ! Scene Plan ! (DeepSeek V4): Ends with a reminder to write the rest of the reply, so DeepSeek V4 stops after the plan less often. On SillyTavern, older plans in context are now replaced with a note telling the model to write a fresh plan. If DeepSeek V4 writes that note in place of its plan, set Prompt Post-Processing to Semi-strict, as the README recommends
  • Missing-block reminders: A block left unclosed, or a reply without the story, now gets a reminder on the next turn. Capitalized or renamed tags (<Status>, <story_threads>, | Phi's Scratchpad | variants) still draw their cards, without false "missing" notices
  • | Story Threads |: A route joins the ledger once the story plants it as a pending hook, and a thread leaves once the story resolves it
  • | Meanwhile | and | Plotlines |: Work with the 「 Conflict 」 tension option
  • Formatting: Asterisks are reserved for one-word emphasis, and ! Force Formatting ! matches the other asterisk rules
  • | Dialogue Color |: Every speaker's color stays readable: very dark, near-white and neon picks shift to a readable shade of the same hue, and colors already easy to read stay as they are. Dialogue colors from a card or extension no longer bleed out of the Scene Plan card
  • Tracker: The scene card shows when the weather has no temperature (deep space, N/A), and long weather notes wrap at phone width instead of covering the clock
  • Faster display: Cards render without lag on long, streaming or unfinished replies, on every host
  • Tracker snow: Falls as varied flakes with a gentle sway, and no longer looks like a repeating grid
  • Colors and ornaments: Amethyst is a deeper purple, and a cut-gem mark replaces the ✦
  • Lumiverse samplers: Top K and Repetition Penalty ship unset, which fixes "Unsupported request field" errors on OpenAI and other strict providers. The README explains when a sampler counts as off
  • ⚠️ README Model Setup ⚠️: GLM 5.3 plans with ! Scene Plan ! (reasoning low). On NanoGPT, pick plain GLM 5.3, because the Thinking variant ignores reasoning effort and overthinks. Mimo 2.6 added. Gemma 4 31b Top K corrected to 64
  • Summaryception prompt: Rewritten for every current module. It keeps your turns and summarizes only the story and the Tracker

V3.0

26 October 2026

Release on GitHub

New

  • The Lumiverse config page. It covers every section, and the whole preset is set up from it, Planning, Content Policy and Phi's Vow included.
  • A new look. Drawn icons and a color for each module. Every footer sits in one Active Modules tray that folds away, with a tab for each section. Characters, Phi, Threads, Plotlines, Meanwhile, Truths, Conflict, Momentum. The Characters tab gathers each character's Status, Relationship, Psychology and Undercurrent rows into one block, and a cast row shows one character at a time. Tracker locations read as breadcrumbs.
  • Animated Tracker scenes, chosen by the setting.
  • Two optional switches, regex, off by default. Start the tray folded, or hide the Tracker scene where it doesn't suit the setting.
  • Meanwhile. What characters and events are doing off the scene.
  • Undercurrents. Tracks what each character wants, what presses on the scene and your own actions, then offers routes from quiet to scene-changing. It replaces Momentum Engine as the default.
  • Phi and her vow. They replace Reinforcement.
  • Phi's Scratchpad, off by default. After the story, Phi names her two weakest lines, each from a different module, and how she'll fix them in her next reply.
  • Talk to Phi. Write out of character, or mention her name, and Phi answers you herself.
  • Hidden Truths. Who knows what, the secrets each character keeps, and from whom.
  • Companions. Phi's friends read the story as she writes it and react below it. Chi, Lou, Sam, Grace, Layla and Phi, with Sola as a guest from Pyropia's Sola preset. Notes mode gives each a short section. Chat mode is a group chat where they answer each other.
  • Relationships, with a score in the tray. Friendship, Realistic Romance, Bubbly Romance and Dark Romance, and arcs like enemies to lovers. Everyday warmth barely moves a score. A milestone moves it a lot.
  • Status, Present Only. Lists only the characters in the scene, so lorebook entries keyed to a name stop firing every turn. A character who leaves gets one "Departed" row and returns with the last known score.
  • Score changes. Status and Relationships show how far a score moved since the last turn, as in 45/100 −5.
  • Psychological States, reworked. Each character's psychology turn by turn. How they read what happened, what they felt, what it made them do. That state carries into the next turn and shapes how they act and speak.
  • Phi's Wanderlust. A Narration option in Phi's own voice, authorial and curious.
  • Lite. A Narration option that keeps the narration rules at the lowest token cost.
  • Three Narrative Styles. Intimate, Melancholic and Suspenseful. Melancholic blends with Comedic into Tragicomedy, and with Lighthearted into Bittersweet.
  • Dialogue. A new Anime option. Heightened is now Theatrical.
  • Dialogue Sounds, off by default. Drawn-Out Words ("pleaaase"), Stammers ("I-I didn't"), Shouting in Caps ("I said STOP.") and Vocal Sounds ("huh?", "pfft"), in any mix, with any Dialogue option. The Scene Plan decides which sounds each character makes that turn.
  • Extra Long. A Length option of 700 to 1000 words.
  • Passthrough, off by default. A note for Gemini 4 Argon, whose safety filter can block safe replies, even in SFW scenes. Turn it on only if your replies get blocked.
  • Formatting checks. If a module or the dialogue colors go missing, a notice appears and the next turn enforces the format. If you turned the module on after that reply, just continue.
  • Choices on Lumiverse. The + buttons combine up to three choices.

Fixed and improved

  • Dialogue Color is more consistent.
  • Redacted routes are fixed.
  • Expressive Dialogue acts each emotion out with better animations. On Lumiverse, asterisks and line breaks inside it render correctly.
  • Narration. Cinematic is the camera and Literary is the mind, now kept apart. Literary is neutral in tone, and its wry voice moved to the Comedic style.
  • First-person character POV works.
  • Your character's pronouns come from your persona first, then from the character sheets, so the model is less likely to misgender you. Lumiverse also reads the persona's pronoun field.
  • Your agency. The characters and the world drive each turn. Your stated actions play out in full, and the model never retells or retries them, or adds your dialogue, decisions, thoughts or feelings.
  • Combat Writing. Your stated moves play out, foes act and their blows can land, and then the turn ends for your answer. Blocks, dodges, counters and finishing blows are yours to call. With Anti-Positivity Bias on, you can be taken down or killed when the fight earns it.
  • Input Improver. A turn of several beats, such as talk, act, talk, keeps its order across paragraphs. Your lines stay quoted speech, and an action-only turn gets no added dialogue.
  • Choices. The action you choose plays out in order, spread across the reply.
  • Visual Storytelling draws a visual only when the story shows something new.
  • Scene Plan. Its rows were rewritten, so the model reads each scene better.
  • The OOC plan row appears only when you step out of character.
  • Formatting breaks less often, and the cards still render when the model slips.
  • Display. Messages render faster, with less lag in long chats.
  • Post-History Instructions, off by default. Your POV, Tense and Asterisks picks win over the card's post-history instructions. On Lumiverse they are an option now, and go before the Response Directives instead of at the very end.
  • Realistic Smut, consent. Characters are less likely to hover and hold back when they should act.
  • Realistic Smut and Max Lewdness. Characters act on their own kinks, and on the ones they know you enjoy.
  • True Thoughts, Visible, no longer colors dialogue when Dialogue Color is off.
  • Change Language. Cards and trackers keep working in another language.
  • Warning System. Setup problems show in a Preset setup card that names the entry and the fix. The model no longer has to write the warning.
  • SillyTavern. If the Experimental Macro Engine is off, a card tells you how to turn it on.
  • Tavo. Long replies no longer risk blanking the chat.
  • Tooltips are rewritten in plain words.
  • Model setup has a new section for Gemini 4 Argon.

V2.5

19 September 2026

Release on GitHub

New

  • Plotlines, an add-on.
  • Expressive Dialogue, under Visuals.
  • A display for Thinking (Fallback).

Fixed and improved

  • Momentum Engine leaves room for calmer moments and routes that let the scene breathe. The model sees only the primary and fallback routes from earlier turns, so it stops offering the same routes in a new order.
  • Response order. The model is told plainly to write every module and the prose in their order, so Scene Plan no longer comes back as the whole reply. DeepSeek V4 0813 was the model it hit hardest.
  • The module displays are tidier and clearer, with less repetition.
  • Reinforcement replaces Supersede. It holds up better against refusals, and the model is less likely to invent a policy it thinks it must follow.
  • Status lets minor characters go sooner.
  • Asterisks have their own option now, apart from POV and tense, and the model follows both more closely.
  • Dialogue length. With Naturalistic dialogue, the default, a line runs longer when the moment needs it.
  • The Tracker leaves out the era in modern settings.
  • Banned Terms covers more of Gemini's stock phrases.
  • Laid-Back pacing is slower, as it was meant to be.
  • World-Building and Combat Writing each have a row in Scene Plan and Thinking (Fallback), so the model weighs them and follows them better.
  • Dialogue Color fails less often. Several safeguards keep the colors steady as the chat grows.
  • Module templates are a little easier for the model, so it makes fewer formatting mistakes, and the fallback displays and plain text turn up less often.
  • The Warning System words its warnings more clearly.

Later updates

  • Update 1. DEUS EX MACHINA V2.6 for Lumiverse fixes an import problem V2.5 had on Lumiverse's staging branch.
  • Update 2, for Lumiverse, still named V2.6. The prose spacing is fixed, and the Choices List under Agency has choices you can click.
  • Update 2.1, for Lumiverse. The preset shows a cover image, loaded from the GitHub repository.

V2.0

28 August 2026

Release on GitHub

New

  • Scene Plan, under Reasoning.
  • Pacing, with its own options.
  • Combat Writing.
  • World-Building.
  • Psychological States, an add-on.
  • Nine Narrative Styles.
  • Input Improver, under Agency.
  • Fandom, under User Utility.
  • Exact Time, an add-on.
  • Nothing Explicit, an Adult option.

Fixed and improved

  • Formatting. Some models get the format slightly wrong, a missing or an extra comma for example. The regex now accepts more variations, and each module display has a fallback, so plain text turns up far less. The model writes dialogue colors in modern CSS, and the regex turns them back into the older font color tag that SillyTavern recognizes. Tavo reads the CSS as it is.
  • Momentum Engine no longer forces a rhythm. It used to push an event into every moment, quiet ones too, and could never ease off. It follows the Pacing rules now, with new wording.
  • The prose, narration and dialogue alike, is more consistent, and each character's voice is more distinct.
  • Length. New ranges and wording keep it more consistent. Long runs longer and writes several linked actions.
  • Visual Storytelling is more consistent, with firmer checks in Scene Plan and the Response Directives.

Later updates

  • Update 2.2. The regex runs faster. With DeepSeek V4 Pro, Scene Plan no longer comes back now and then as the whole reply.
  • Update 2.3. The Status display's regex no longer slows the chat.
